Welcome to HSOS, the operating environment of Hiraeth Systems. You are logged in as employee
(uid 1000, group: assets). Underlined text opens the item it names. Begin with
[/srv/divisions](hsos:open/divisions), which lists the Corporation's current product lines.
You may also tune into [/dev/lp7](hsos:open/listening), inspect the
[ledger](hsos:open/ledger) or consult [The Board](hsos:open/board).

## §1 Getting started

- A [terminal](hsos:open/terminal) is provided. Type `help` for a list of permitted commands.
- Press `F7` in the terminal to receive a statement from the Board.
- This workstation supports 256 colours. Requests for more must be made in writing.

## §2 Filesystem orientation

- `/home/employee`: your home directory. Contents remain Corporation property.
- `/srv/divisions`: product lines, filed by division.
- `/opt/board`: the Board.
- `/proc/longing`: current longing, per core.
- `/var/log/morale.log`: morale, logged hourly.
- `/mnt/legacy`: HSOS 1.0. Still mounted.
- `/dev/null`: feedback.
