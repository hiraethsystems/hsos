Flare Enough is a free, open source Android app for tracking medication, logging symptoms and
taking a few calm minutes. It is built for people with chronic illness. The name is a pun on
flares and "fair enough".

Version 0.1.5 is the current release. It runs on Android 8.0 and newer.

## What it promises

- No ads, no subscriptions and no in app purchases.
- No analytics, no trackers and no accounts.
- No internet permission at all. Your data stays on your phone unless you export or back it up
  yourself.
- Reminders that arrive on time, including when the phone is idle, after a reboot, and across
  the clock changes.
- A calm interface designed for bad days: sore hands, fatigue, brain fog and anxiety.

## What it does

- **Medication**: flexible schedules, from every day to cycles or as needed. A reminder health
  screen shows whether your phone's settings will let reminders through, and offers a one minute
  test. Missed doses are recorded gently, with no red warnings.
- **Symptoms**: simple trackers you name yourself, a flare flag for the day, a "same as
  yesterday" shortcut, and a body map for where it hurts. An optional period tracker records
  days and flow and never predicts anything.
- **History**: a month calendar, a plain timeline for each day, and the option to go back and
  correct any past day.
- **Stillness**: a timer, gentle guided breathing, a soft bell, and room for your own prompts and
  readings.
- **Your data**: back up and restore to a single file, export a CSV or a plain PDF report to show
  a clinician, and lock the app with your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN unlock.
- Light and dark themes, large touch targets, full TalkBack support, and a layout that holds up
  with the largest system text.

## Not medical advice

Flare Enough records and reminds only. It does not diagnose, suggest treatments, change doses,
check drug interactions or interpret symptoms.

## Installing

Download the signed APK from the latest release and open it on your phone. Android will ask you
to allow installing from that source the first time. An F-Droid listing is in progress. Licensed
under the GNU GPL v3 or later.
